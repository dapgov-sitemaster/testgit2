Daily Dog Care Routine

Keeping your pup happy and healthy requires simple daily care. Use our interactive checklist to track your pup's routine today!

Today's Care Progress 0%

Check off tasks to complete your daily dog care routine!

1. Balanced Nutrition & Fresh Water
Provide high-quality age-appropriate dog food in measured portions. Always keep clean, fresh drinking water accessible throughout the day. Clean bowls daily to prevent bacteria.
2. Daily Physical Exercise
Aim for at least 30 to 60 minutes of daily physical activity tailored to your dog’s breed. Brisk walks, fetch games, or park visits burn off extra energy and maintain joint health.
3. Mental Stimulation & Enrichment
A tired mind is a calm mind! Use treat-dispensing puzzle toys, short trick-training sessions, or scent games to prevent boredom and destructive behaviors.
4. Coat Grooming & Dental Health
Brush loose fur regularly to prevent painful matting. Brush their teeth using dog-safe toothpaste or offer dental chews to keep gums healthy and breath fresh.
